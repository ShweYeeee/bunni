ADOPTING A FRIEND

Our adoption procedure includes a phone interview, home visit, and any necessary education of potential adopters. There is an adoption fee to help defray the cost of veterinary care and spay/neuter of the bunnies we find homes for. For more information about adoptions, please email us.

MEETING THE RABBITS

We don’t have a physical shelter building. The rabbits are fostered in our homes. If you are interested in any particular bunny (or bunnies), we will make arrangements for you to meet them once you are approved to adopt. If you would like a companion for a bunny you already have, we will arrange for several “meetings” and a bonding session to ensure your bunny will be happy with his/her new mate!

Our Mission

At Bunny Haven Rescue, our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and neglected rabbits. We are dedicated to educating the public about proper rabbit care and promoting compassionate treatment for all animals.

Our mission is to rescue and care for rabbits in need, providing them with a safe and loving environment. We are committed to promoting rabbit welfare and finding forever homes for our rescued rabbits.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by a group of passionate animal lovers, Bunny Haven began as a small foster network. Over the years, we have grown into a full rescue organization, helping hundreds of rabbits find safe, loving homes.

Did you know? Rabbits are the third most abandoned pet in shelters. Our work helps reduce this number through rescue and education.

What We Do

  • Rescue rabbits from shelters and unsafe environments
  • Provide medical care and rehabilitation
  • Match rabbits with loving adoptive families
  • Offer education on proper rabbit care
